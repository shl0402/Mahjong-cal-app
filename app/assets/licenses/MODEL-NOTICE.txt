RECOGNITION LIMITS
Input: float32 RGB 1 x 3 x 640 x 640, aspect-preserving letterbox, padding114.
Output: float32 1 x 46 x 8400, four box channels then42 class scores.
B=bamboo, C=characters, D=circles. Eight flower/season classes are retained as
unsupported tile -1, preventing automatic stable acceptance. No distinct
red-five or general UNKNOWN class exists. Jokers, backs and other unsupported
faces are not reliably rejected merely because they lack a model class.
The same confidence, count, geometry and temporal checks remain in use.
High-confidence wrong predictions were observed. Every hand needs the user's
visual confirmation/correction. Development results are not universal accuracy.
The historical public-video replay uses the old YOLO11n V2 predictions and
does not establish current AR42 video behavior. Recognition and scoring differ.
